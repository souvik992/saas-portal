Extend restaurant ordering beyond the dining room

Customers may want to place an order without visiting the restaurant or opening a third-party marketplace. A mobile ordering channel gives them another way to browse the menu and send an order to the business. For the restaurant, the important requirement is that app orders remain part of the same operational process used by staff to receive, prepare and complete orders.

POSINEXT App Ordering is designed as a customer-facing channel connected with the restaurant's configured POS workflow. During a demo, review the customer journey and the staff view together—not just the app screens.

Follow the order from menu to kitchen

Evaluate the complete lifecycle: a customer opens the app, explores categories and items, selects available options, submits an order, and the outlet receives it for handling. Ask the team to demonstrate how an order appears to restaurant staff and how it proceeds into the kitchen preparation workflow. Also confirm how the restaurant handles unavailable items, order changes and customer questions.

App ordering and website ordering serve different touchpoints

A mobile app provides an installed app experience for customers who choose to use it. A browser-based restaurant ordering website gives customers a web destination without requiring an app installation. The two can complement each other, provided menus, order handling and customer support are kept consistent.

For an overview of the branded customer app option, see POSINEXT Food Delivery App. For in-restaurant mobile ordering, explore Table QR.

Plan the mobile ordering experience

  • Identify the locations and customers the app is intended to serve.
  • Review how menu categories, item options and availability will be maintained.
  • Test order submission and confirm how the outlet receives each order.
  • Define the restaurant's process for pickup, delivery and customer enquiries.
  • Confirm app distribution, device support and ongoing ownership responsibilities before rollout.

Restaurant app ordering FAQs

What is app ordering for a restaurant?

It is a customer-facing mobile app channel through which guests can browse a restaurant menu and submit orders.

Can mobile app orders connect to restaurant POS software?

POSINEXT App Ordering is designed to connect customer orders with the restaurant's POS workflow. Ask for a live walkthrough of order receipt and kitchen handling for your configuration.

Should a restaurant have both an ordering app and website?

They serve different customer touchpoints. A website is accessible in a browser, while an app provides an app-based experience. The right mix depends on your audience and operating model.
