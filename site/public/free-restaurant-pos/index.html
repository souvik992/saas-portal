Free GST billing software

GST bills with CGST and SGST, inclusive or exclusive prices, UPI QR on the bill, cash, card and split payments, held bills and discounts.

Free KOT printing

Kitchen order tickets go to the right station's printer by themselves, with change and cancel slips. 58 and 80 mm thermal printers.

Free kitchen display (KDS)

Tickets on a kitchen screen with New, Ready and All-day views; late tickets turn amber, then red, with a chime for new orders.

Free offline billing

FAQ

Free restaurant POS: questions, answered

Is POSINEXT really a free restaurant POS?

Yes. The Free plan costs ₹0 and has no end date: every feature on one billing PC per outlet, with no limit on bills, menu items or sales. You start with a 30-day trial and then stay on the Free plan by yourself; nothing locks and you never enter a card.

What is the catch with the free POS?

There is no catch in the software: billing, KOT, kitchen display, Table QR, captain app, Swiggy and Zomato, stock and reports are all included. The Free plan is for one billing PC (plus one captain phone and one kitchen screen) and self-service setup with help-centre and email support. Paid plans add more PCs, installation and training visits and priority support. See pricing.

Which is the best free restaurant POS software in India?

Look for a free POS that prints GST-compliant bills, sends KOTs to the kitchen, keeps billing when the internet is down and does not cap your bills, menu or turnover. POSINEXT's Free plan does all four, and adds Table QR ordering, a captain app and Swiggy and Zomato orders.

Is there free restaurant billing software with GST?

Yes. POSINEXT prints GST bills with CGST and SGST (or IGST), your GSTIN and FSSAI number, gap-free bill numbers per financial year and GST-by-rate reports, on the Free plan.

Can I download a free POS for Windows?

Yes. Sign up free, then download POSINEXT for Windows 10 or 11 from your account. It installs like any Windows app and works offline.

Does the free POS work without internet?

Yes. Billing, KOTs and reports run on your own PC, so bills keep printing when the internet is down. Online orders and online backups catch up when it is back.

Is it free for cafés, QSRs and cloud kitchens too?

Yes. Choose QSR (quickPOS) for counters, cafés, bakeries and cloud kitchens, or Fine Dine (DinePOS) for table service. Both are on the Free plan, and you can use both.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC or laptop and a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt printer (USB, LAN, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth). An Android phone for the captain app and any screen for the kitchen display are optional.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Nothing stops. Your POS moves to the Free plan by itself, with every feature on one PC, and keeps renewing while the PC goes online now and then. You only pay if you choose a plan for more PCs or support.

Is POSINEXT a free alternative to Petpooja?

POSINEXT has a free plan with every feature on one PC, and its paid plans are priced 10% below Petpooja's published rates. See POSINEXT vs Petpooja.
