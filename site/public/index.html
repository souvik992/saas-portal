Frequently asked questions

Restaurant POS software FAQs

What is restaurant POS software?

Restaurant POS software helps manage orders, billing, payments and service workflows. POSINEXT connects those workflows with kitchen tickets, table ordering and digital sales channels.

Can POSINEXT be used for fine-dine and QSR restaurants?

Yes. POSINEXT provides workflows for fine-dine restaurants, quick-service restaurants and cloud kitchens, with additional ordering products that can be configured around the outlet's operating model.

Does POSINEXT support kitchen order tickets?

POSINEXT connects order capture with kitchen order ticket workflows and kitchen display processes, helping front-of-house and kitchen teams work from the same order information.

Can I accept online orders through POSINEXT?

POSINEXT offers Table QR, website ordering, app ordering and supported Swiggy and Zomato integration options. Exact availability depends on the outlet's integration setup.

How do I get POSINEXT pricing?

Pricing depends on outlets, terminals, service model and selected add-ons. Contact the POSINEXT team for a proposal based on your requirements.
