FAQ

Restaurant POS questions, answered

What is POSINEXT?

POSINEXT is restaurant POS software made for India: billing, KOTs, table management, Table QR ordering, a captain app, kitchen display, online ordering (website, app, Swiggy and Zomato) and reports, for fine dine restaurants, QSRs, cafés and cloud kitchens.

Does POSINEXT work without internet?

Yes. The POS runs on your outlet's Windows PC, so billing, KOTs, the captain app and kitchen screens keep working on your local network when the internet is down. Online orders and backups catch up when it is back.

How much does POSINEXT cost?

Plans are ₹10,800, ₹18,000 and ₹27,000 per outlet per year plus GST, with a 30-day free trial. See pricing.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 30 days, with no card. Create an account, download the POS and start billing.

Does POSINEXT integrate with Swiggy and Zomato?

Yes. Swiggy and Zomato orders arrive in the POS, are accepted automatically and print as KOTs. See Swiggy & Zomato integration.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and a 58 or 80 mm thermal printer. Captains use Android phones; kitchen screens can be any PC or screen on the network. See the documentation.
