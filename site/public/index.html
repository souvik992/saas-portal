One restaurant POS platform for different service models

A restaurant does not operate through billing alone. Staff take orders, kitchens prepare them, managers follow service and customers increasingly use digital channels. POSINEXT is designed to connect those touchpoints without forcing every restaurant into the same service workflow.

Fine Dine POS supports table-led service, floor visibility, order taking and split billing. QSR POS focuses on counter ordering, menu-led billing and held bills. Cloud Kitchen brings digital orders and kitchen preparation into a connected workflow.

Connect the front of house with the kitchen

When an order is captured at a counter, table or digital channel, the next step is kitchen preparation. POSINEXT connects supported order flows to kitchen order tickets (KOTs) and preparation workflows. This gives front-of-house and kitchen teams a shared view of what has been ordered and what needs attention.

  • Capture dine-in or counter orders in the relevant POS workflow.
  • Send order details into the configured KOT and kitchen preparation flow.
  • Keep online and in-store order channels connected to outlet operations where supported.

Give customers more ways to order

Build on the core POS with customer and staff-facing ordering channels. Guests can scan a Table QR code to open the menu and place an order. Service staff can use the Captain App to take orders at the table. Restaurants can also explore a branded food ordering website, mobile app ordering and a restaurant delivery app. Supported Swiggy and Zomato orders can be connected to the POS workflow.

Outlet operations with connected cloud services

The outlet POS is designed for Windows and supports offline operation for supported local workflows. POSINEXT Cloud supports online orders, backups and licences, while the customer portal gives customers a place to access their account, manage licences and open support requests. Internet-dependent cloud features still require connectivity, so review the exact offline and sync behaviour for your setup during a demo.

How to choose the right restaurant POS

Start with the way your team works today. Map the path from order entry to kitchen ticket, bill and payment. Then test the scenarios that matter: a busy counter queue, a table split across guests, a modifier-heavy order, a cancelled item, an internet interruption and an incoming online order. Compare systems on the completeness of those workflows rather than on a feature list alone.

Frequently asked questions

What types of restaurants can use POSINEXT?

POSINEXT has workflows for fine-dine restaurants, quick-service restaurants and cloud kitchens. The right configuration depends on service style, outlets, terminals and the modules required.

Does the outlet POS work without the internet?

The Windows outlet POS is designed to continue working offline for supported local workflows. Online orders and cloud services require connectivity. Ask the team to demonstrate the precise offline and sync behaviour relevant to your operation.

Can POSINEXT connect with Swiggy and Zomato?

POSINEXT provides supported Swiggy and Zomato order integration. Availability and the exact workflow depend on the integration setup and account configuration; confirm these details during a demo.

How much does restaurant POS software cost?

Pricing depends on your outlets, terminals, service model and selected add-ons. Visit the pricing page or contact the team for a proposal based on your requirements.
