Restaurant POS features that connect service, kitchen and ordering

A useful restaurant POS does more than print a bill. POSINEXT brings together point-of-sale billing, table service, kitchen order tickets and digital ordering channels so restaurant teams can manage connected workflows.

Point-of-sale billing for different restaurant formats

Fine-dine and quick-service teams do not take orders in the same way. POSINEXT provides a Fine Dine POS workflow for table visibility, waiter order entry and split bills, alongside a QSR POS workflow for menu-led counter orders and held bills. Choose the service model that reflects how guests order at your outlet.

Kitchen order tickets and preparation workflows

Orders need to reach the people preparing them with the right details. POSINEXT connects order capture to kitchen order ticket (KOT) workflows. During a product demo, review how items, modifiers and order updates appear in the configured kitchen flow, including the stations and printers or displays used by your team.

Table and mobile order-taking options

Restaurants can extend the point of sale with different ordering experiences. Table QR allows guests to scan a code at the table, view the menu and submit orders. The Captain App supports staff-led mobile order entry at a table. Both should be evaluated against the restaurant's service style and guest expectations.

Direct digital ordering and aggregator channels

POSINEXT includes options for a restaurant's own online ordering website, app ordering and branded food delivery app. Supported Swiggy and Zomato order integration can bring aggregator orders into the POS workflow. Confirm integration availability, account prerequisites and the exact order lifecycle for your setup before purchase.

Windows outlet POS and connected cloud services

The outlet POS is designed to run on Windows and supports offline operation for supported local workflows. POSINEXT Cloud supports online orders, backups and licences. Online services depend on internet connectivity, so ask the team to demonstrate how local billing and cloud-dependent functions behave when the connection is interrupted.

Which features should you evaluate first?

  • Order entry: how staff select items, variants and modifiers.
  • Kitchen handoff: how KOTs are produced and routed.
  • Billing: how taxes, discounts, split bills and payments work for your cases.
  • Digital ordering: how QR, website, app and aggregator orders reach the outlet.
  • Operations: what continues offline and what requires cloud connectivity.

Restaurant POS features FAQs

Does one POS support fine dine and QSR?

POSINEXT has separate workflows for fine-dine table service and QSR counter service. The team can help you review which configuration and modules suit your business.

Does POSINEXT include online ordering?

POSINEXT offers a set of customer ordering channels, including Table QR, website ordering and app ordering, as well as supported aggregator integration. Confirm the channels required for your outlet.

Can the POS work offline?

The Windows outlet POS supports offline operation for supported local workflows. Cloud-dependent features, including online orders, require connectivity.
