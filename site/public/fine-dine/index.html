Keep every table visible during service

In a fine-dining restaurant, the cashier and service team need more than a list of open bills. They need to know which tables are occupied, which orders are in progress and which guests are ready to close. A live floor-plan workflow helps staff understand the dining room at a glance and move between tables without losing track of the service.

POSINEXT Fine Dine brings table status and running order activity into the POS. During your demo, use your own floor layout and table naming so the team can assess how the workflow fits your seating arrangement.

Capture waiter orders and send clear kitchen tickets

Waiters need to record items and modifiers accurately while speaking with guests. POSINEXT supports order entry for table service and routes captured order details into kitchen order ticket (KOT) workflows. This connects the front of house to the kitchen and gives preparation teams an order record to work from.

For mobile order taking at the table, review the POSINEXT Captain App. It is designed to help service staff select a table, choose menu items and modifiers, and submit the order into the restaurant workflow.

Handle the bill at the end of the meal

Guests may want to settle one bill together or divide it across people. A fine-dine POS should make the close-out process as clear as the order-taking process. POSINEXT supports split-bill and payment collection workflows for dine-in operations. Review the exact payment methods and settlement scenarios your restaurant needs before selecting a configuration.

Extend table service with digital ordering

Fine-dine operations can add customer-facing ordering options without losing the connection to the outlet POS. Table QR ordering lets guests scan a table code to browse the menu and submit orders. Staff can continue to take orders through the POS or Captain App. These channels are part of the wider restaurant POS platform.

What to test in a fine-dine POS demo

  • Set up a representative floor plan and open several tables.
  • Add items and modifiers, then verify how KOTs reach the relevant kitchen workflow.
  • Review table status changes and how staff find an active order.
  • Split a bill and walk through the payment methods your outlet accepts.
  • Test how Table QR or mobile waiter ordering fits alongside regular POS order entry.

Fine-dine POS FAQs

What is fine-dine POS software?

It is restaurant point-of-sale software built around table service. In addition to billing, it typically needs table visibility, waiter order entry, kitchen tickets and flexible bill settlement.

Can POSINEXT manage tables and floor plans?

Yes. POSINEXT Fine Dine includes a live floor-plan and table-status workflow. Ask for a walkthrough using the layout and table flow of your restaurant.

Does Fine Dine support KOTs?

POSINEXT connects order entry with kitchen order ticket workflows. Confirm the kitchen stations, printers or display setup required for your outlet.
