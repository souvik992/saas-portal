Let customers order from your restaurant website

A restaurant website can do more than display the address, menu and contact details. With online ordering, it becomes another way for customers to choose items and submit an order. A branded ordering website gives the restaurant control over its own customer-facing destination and can complement orders received over the phone, at the counter or through third-party marketplaces.

POSINEXT Website Ordering connects that customer journey with the configured restaurant POS workflow. The goal is to avoid treating website orders as a separate process that staff must manually recreate.

Connect the menu experience with order fulfilment

Before launching, check the full path from menu browsing to order submission and kitchen preparation. Confirm how items and options are displayed, where orders appear for staff, how the kitchen receives order details and how the outlet handles a change or cancellation. The exact settings and customer journey should be confirmed for your configuration.

Build a direct channel alongside dine-in and delivery

Direct website ordering can sit alongside Table QR ordering for in-store guests and a branded food delivery app for app users. Restaurants that receive third-party orders can also review supported Swiggy and Zomato integration. These channels work best when the outlet has a clear process for receiving and preparing every order.

Restaurant website ordering launch checklist

  • Confirm the brand name, domain and customer-facing contact details.
  • Review menu categories, item descriptions, prices and available options.
  • Test order submission on desktop and mobile browsers.
  • Verify how website orders appear at the outlet and reach the kitchen.
  • Explain delivery area, pickup, operating hours and customer support clearly on the site.

Restaurant online ordering FAQs

What is a restaurant online ordering website?

It is a restaurant-branded website that allows customers to browse a menu and submit an order online, rather than relying only on phone orders or third-party marketplaces.

Do website orders reach the restaurant POS?

POSINEXT Website Ordering is designed to connect website orders with the restaurant's configured POS workflow. Confirm the order lifecycle and kitchen handoff in a demo.

Can a restaurant use a website and mobile app together?

Yes. A restaurant can consider a browser-based ordering website and a separate mobile app experience. Review POSINEXT App Ordering for the app channel.
