How QR code ordering works at a restaurant

A table QR ordering system gives each dining table a scannable entry point to the restaurant's digital menu. A guest opens the phone camera, scans the code and views the menu without searching for a printed card or installing a separate app just to read it. With POSINEXT Table QR, the guest can also submit an order from the table, and that order joins the restaurant's configured POS workflow.

The goal is not to remove hospitality from table service. It is to give guests another convenient way to browse and order while allowing staff to focus on service, questions and special requests.

Keep digital orders connected to restaurant operations

A QR menu is most useful when the order does not become a separate task for the team. POSINEXT connects Table QR orders with the restaurant's order workflow so staff and kitchen teams can handle submitted items alongside other orders. Review the exact order acceptance, KOT and preparation steps in a demo to confirm that the flow matches your outlet.

Where table QR ordering can help

  • Restaurants that want guests to browse the current menu on their phone.
  • Busy dining rooms where guests may prefer to place an order directly.
  • Restaurants that want to add a digital ordering option alongside waiter-led service.
  • Operators who want QR orders connected to the outlet POS rather than managed as a separate channel.

For staff-led mobile order entry, compare the Captain App. For the wider POS workflow, explore Fine Dine POS.

Plan a table QR rollout

Before printing or placing codes, decide how tables will be named, where the QR codes will be displayed and how staff will assist guests who need help. Test the guest journey on both Android and iPhone devices, check menu readability, and place a test order from several tables. Also confirm the handling of out-of-stock items, menu changes and order corrections with the restaurant team.

Restaurant QR ordering FAQs

Do customers need to install an app to scan the QR menu?

The Table QR flow is designed for guests to scan the table code and open the digital menu on their phone. Confirm supported devices and browser requirements during your product walkthrough.

Can guests place orders through the QR menu?

Yes. POSINEXT Table QR supports viewing the menu and submitting orders from the table. The submitted order joins the restaurant's configured order workflow.

Does a QR order reach the kitchen?

Table QR orders are connected to the POSINEXT order workflow. Review the exact acceptance and KOT routing steps for your kitchen configuration during a demo.
