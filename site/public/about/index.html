About

Restaurant software that never stops

We build POS software for Indian restaurants that keeps billing through internet outages, rush hours and everything in between.

What POSINEXT is

POSINEXT is restaurant software made for how restaurants in India work: GST bills, UPI at the counter, KOTs to busy kitchens, captains on the floor, and Swiggy, Zomato and direct orders arriving all at once.

It comes as two POS products, each built for its job: Fine Dine for table service and QSR for counter service, cafés and cloud kitchens. Add-ons such as Table QR, the captain app, a kitchen display and online ordering work with both, through PosiNext Cloud.

What we believe

  • The restaurant never stops for the internet. The POS runs on the outlet's own PC; the cloud adds online orders, backups and remote access, but billing never waits for it.
  • No order, ticket or rupee gets lost. Print queues that never drop a KOT, phones that keep orders through Wi-Fi drops, and a leakage dashboard that shows every cancellation and void.
  • Fair, published prices. Per outlet, per year, no setup fees, and a 30-day free trial to prove it.
  • Direct orders belong to the restaurant. Your own website, app and Table QR, without commission on each order.
