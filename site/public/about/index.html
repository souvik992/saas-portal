About POSINEXT

A connected operating system for modern restaurants

POSINEXT brings point-of-sale billing, kitchen workflows and digital ordering together so restaurant teams can manage service from one connected platform.

Built around the restaurant workflow

Restaurant service spans the counter or dining room, kitchen, delivery channels and customer ordering experience. POSINEXT brings these touchpoints together through Fine Dine and QSR point-of-sale products plus connected tools for Table QR, captain ordering, kitchen display and online orders.

At the front of house

Fine Dine supports a live floor plan, table status, running totals, station-routed KOTs and split bills. QSR supports fast counter billing, held bills and cash, UPI and card payment workflows.

In the kitchen

Kitchen tickets and kitchen display workflows help teams see preparation, ready and completed orders. Multi-brand cloud kitchen operations can route orders into a shared kitchen workflow.

Across ordering channels

Table QR, Captain App, restaurant website and app ordering, and Swiggy and Zomato integration connect guest ordering with the POS and kitchen.

Designed for real outlet conditions

The Windows POS is designed to work offline at the outlet. Outlets connect to POSINEXT Cloud for online orders, backups and licences. This separates local day-to-day billing from cloud-connected services that need an internet connection.

Products in the POSINEXT ecosystem

  • Fine Dine (DinePOS) for table-service restaurants.
  • QSR (Tiffin POS) for quick-service restaurants, cafés and counter operations.
  • Table QR and Captain App for guest self-ordering and waiter order taking.
  • Cloud Kitchen and Kitchen Display for preparation workflows.
  • Website Ordering, App Ordering and Food Delivery App for direct digital ordering.
  • Swiggy and Zomato integration through the online order workflow.
