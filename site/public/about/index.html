About POSINEXT

One connected platform for modern restaurant operations

POSINEXT brings point-of-sale billing, kitchen workflows and customer ordering channels together, helping restaurant teams manage service from one connected system.

Get startedTalk to our team

For the front of house

Table visibility, billing and mobile order taking.

For the kitchen

KOT workflows, kitchen display and incoming online orders.

For direct ordering

Restaurant website and app ordering, alongside supported aggregator channels.

Built around restaurant workflows

POSINEXT supports fine-dine table service, quick-service counter billing and cloud-kitchen operations. The platform connects orders, menus and kitchen activity across supported channels.

Designed for connected operations

The outlet POS is designed to run on Windows and continue working offline. POSINEXT Cloud supports online orders, backups and licences, while the customer portal provides account and licence management.

A connected product ecosystem

The platform includes Fine Dine POS, QSR POS, Table QR, Captain App, Cloud Kitchen, Food Delivery App, Website Ordering, App Ordering and Swiggy & Zomato integration.
