About POSINEXT

Restaurant technology built around connected operations

POSINEXT brings restaurant point-of-sale billing, kitchen workflows and customer ordering channels together. The platform is designed for fine-dine restaurants, quick-service counters and cloud kitchens that want a connected way to manage day-to-day operations.

One platform across the restaurant order journey

Restaurant operations connect several teams. A guest places an order, service staff capture it, the kitchen prepares it and the cashier completes the bill. Online and aggregator orders add more entry points to the same process. POSINEXT is designed to connect these activities through a core POS and a set of restaurant-specific modules.

Designed for different restaurant formats

Fine-dine restaurants need table and floor visibility, waiter order entry and flexible bill settlement. Quick-service businesses need menu-led counter billing and a clear handoff to the kitchen. Cloud kitchens need to coordinate incoming orders and preparation across their brands. POSINEXT provides distinct workflows for these operating models rather than treating every outlet as a single generic checkout counter.

Explore Fine Dine POS, QSR POS and Cloud Kitchen to understand the differences.

Connect in-store service with digital ordering

The platform includes customer and staff ordering touchpoints such as Table QR, the Captain App, a restaurant's own ordering website and app ordering. Supported Swiggy and Zomato integration provides another route for aggregator orders to enter the restaurant workflow.

Outlet software and cloud services

The outlet POS is designed to run on Windows and supports offline operation for supported local workflows. POSINEXT Cloud supports online orders, backups and licences. Customers can use the customer portal to access account and licence features and open support requests. The team can explain which capabilities require internet connectivity for a particular deployment.

How to learn more about POSINEXT

Start with the platform feature overview and pricing information. For a conversation about your outlet, share the service format, outlet count, terminals and the ordering channels you want to connect. The contact page is the starting point for a guided product walkthrough.
