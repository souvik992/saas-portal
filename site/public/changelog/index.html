What’s new in POSINEXT

A summary of shipped product work and platform improvements based on the project release history. Items are grouped by release period rather than representing a complete per-version changelog.

November 2026

Online order and cloud operations

  • Online order alerts and preparation timing improvements, including an alert that continues until an order is accepted.
  • Captain App Android distribution as a Fine Dine add-on.
  • Kitchen Display availability as a Fine Dine add-on.
  • Customer portal improvements for accounts, licences, support tickets, announcements, knowledge base and service status.
  • Installer releases and downloads managed through POSINEXT Cloud.
November 2026

Fine Dine and restaurant operations

  • Floor plan, table states, live order handling and billing workflows.
  • KOT routing, reprints, changes and kitchen station handling.
  • Bill settlement, split bills, void workflows and day-end reporting.
  • Stock, recipes, purchases, wastage and usage reporting.
  • Captain order taking, course-wise firing and offline queue handling.
  • Kitchen display workflows and kitchen speed reporting.
October–November 2026

Online ordering and connected outlets

  • Swiggy and Zomato order integration through the online order hub.
  • Restaurant website ordering, order routing and online order management.
  • Table QR ordering through the order hub.
  • Cloud connectivity for outlets, remote device access and cloud printing support.
  • Online backups and operational monitoring improvements.

This is a curated summary, not a complete version-by-version release record. Availability can vary by product, installer version, plan and outlet configuration. For current downloads, sign in to the customer portal.
