Choosing restaurant POS software

Start with the way your restaurant serves customers. Fine-dine restaurants need table and course workflows; QSR counters need fast billing; cloud kitchens need a clear view of orders from multiple channels. Compare the workflows before comparing feature lists.

Why connect POS and kitchen workflows?

When orders move from the point of sale to the kitchen through a consistent KOT workflow, teams can reduce re-entry and keep front-of-house and kitchen staff aligned.

Direct ordering and customer experience

A restaurant website, branded app or Table QR experience can provide additional ordering touchpoints. The important consideration is keeping menus, pricing and order handling aligned with the outlet POS.
