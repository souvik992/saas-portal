Built around the pace of a quick-service counter

QSR teams often handle a high frequency of short transactions. Staff need to find products quickly, add the right options and complete the bill without navigating a table-service workflow. POSINEXT QSR keeps the emphasis on menu-led order entry and counter billing, making it suitable for quick-service restaurants, fast-food outlets, cafes and takeaway counters.

During a walkthrough, use your actual menu categories—such as meals, snacks, beverages or combos—to see how staff move from item selection to bill completion.

Make menu navigation practical for staff

A counter POS should reflect the menu that employees use every day. A photo-led, category-based menu can make frequently ordered products easier to locate and can reduce the number of screens staff need to navigate. Review product names, variants, modifiers and the order of categories using a representative menu before rollout.

Keep held orders and kitchen preparation connected

Not every order can be completed in one uninterrupted step. A held-bill workflow helps staff return to an order that needs to wait before payment or completion. Once captured, order details can move into the configured kitchen order ticket (KOT) process so kitchen staff can prepare from the same order information used at the counter.

For a more kitchen-focused operating model, explore cloud kitchen workflows. For the complete feature set, visit the restaurant POS features page.

Use a QSR POS demo to validate real counter scenarios

  • Ring up a typical order using your menu categories and frequently sold items.
  • Add variants or modifiers and verify the final bill.
  • Hold an order and retrieve it before completing payment.
  • Send an order to the kitchen and review the KOT workflow.

QSR POS FAQs

What does QSR POS software do?

QSR POS software supports quick counter ordering and billing. Depending on the system, it can also connect counter orders to kitchen tickets, payments and other restaurant order channels.

Is POSINEXT suitable for a small fast-food outlet?

POSINEXT has a QSR workflow for counter-based service. The best configuration depends on the number of terminals, menu complexity and additional modules required.

Can QSR orders go to the kitchen?

POSINEXT connects QSR order entry with kitchen order ticket workflows. Confirm the station routing and printing or display requirements for your setup.
