Why connect Swiggy and Zomato with restaurant POS?

Restaurants that sell through food delivery platforms must handle orders arriving outside the dine-in counter. When staff have to switch between multiple devices or re-enter order details, there are more steps to manage during a busy service. A POS integration aims to bring supported aggregator orders into the restaurant's operating workflow so teams can handle them alongside other orders.

POSINEXT provides supported Swiggy and Zomato integration. The exact availability, partner requirements and order lifecycle should be confirmed for the restaurant's account and selected configuration before rollout.

Connect incoming orders with kitchen tickets

Once an aggregator order is received and accepted, the kitchen needs clear item details and instructions. POSINEXT is designed to connect supported aggregator orders with its restaurant order and kitchen ticket workflows. Ask for a demonstration showing an actual test order from channel receipt through KOT generation and preparation, including how staff identify the order source.

Questions to confirm before enabling an integration

  • Is the restaurant's Swiggy or Zomato partner account eligible for the proposed integration?
  • What account permissions, partner approval or API access is required?
  • How are order acceptance, rejection, cancellation and status updates handled?
  • How are item modifiers, special instructions, taxes and order totals represented?
  • How do aggregator orders reach the KOT or kitchen display workflow?
  • Are menu sync, availability sync, reconciliation or other functions included, or handled separately?

Do not assume every integration includes menu synchronisation, stock synchronisation, payout reconciliation or automatic status updates. Confirm each requirement in writing.

Support delivery orders across restaurant formats

For delivery-focused operators, aggregator order handling can be reviewed alongside Cloud Kitchen. Restaurants that also want their own customer channel can explore website ordering and a branded food delivery app. POSINEXT brings these modules into a broader restaurant POS platform.

Swiggy and Zomato integration FAQs

Can POSINEXT integrate with Swiggy and Zomato?

POSINEXT supports Swiggy and Zomato order integration for supported configurations. Contact the team to verify account eligibility, onboarding prerequisites and exact feature availability.

Do aggregator orders generate kitchen tickets?

Supported aggregator orders connect to POSINEXT's order and kitchen ticket workflow. Confirm station routing and the end-to-end order lifecycle during a demonstration.

Does the integration automatically sync menus and stock?

Those capabilities should not be assumed. Ask the POSINEXT team to confirm menu, availability, stock and reconciliation behaviour for the specific integration being proposed.

Need to verify account eligibility and integration scope? Contact POSINEXT before planning your aggregator rollout.
