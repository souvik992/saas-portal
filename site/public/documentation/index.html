Product documentation

Understand the POSINEXT restaurant workflow

An overview of the main products and operating flows. Exact setup steps and device compatibility should be confirmed for the version and outlet configuration being used.

01 · Point of sale

Fine Dine POS

Use the floor plan to view table states and open orders. Add items, hold or fire them to kitchen stations, move or merge tables, split bills and settle with supported tender types.

02 · Counter service

QSR / Tiffin POS

Use a photo-first menu and category tabs for counter billing. Hold bills, apply the configured billing and tax workflow, take cash, UPI or card and send kitchen tickets.

03 · Kitchen

KOT and Kitchen Display

Kitchen Order Tickets route items to configured stations. Kitchen display workflows show preparation, ready and completed lanes.

04 · Floor team

Captain App

Waiters can take orders from a phone or tablet, work with courses and send order details to the kitchen workflow.

05 · Guest ordering

Table QR

Guests scan a table code to browse a digital menu, place orders and call a waiter.

06 · Online channels

ODS and integrations

Website, app, Swiggy and Zomato orders can be brought into the online ordering workflow and routed to the outlet POS and kitchen.

Offline operation and cloud services

The Windows POS at the outlet is designed to continue local billing when internet connectivity is unavailable. Online orders, cloud backups, account operations and licence services depend on connectivity to POSINEXT Cloud.

Getting started

  1. Create an account in the customer portal.
  2. Choose the restaurant type and product(s) for your outlet.
  3. Use the portal to access available installers and start the 30-day trial.
  4. Configure your outlet, menu, taxes, kitchen stations and printers before live service.

For step-by-step assistance, open a support ticket in the customer portal or contact the team.
