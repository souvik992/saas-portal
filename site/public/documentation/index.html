Point of sale

Fine Dine supports table-based service and billing. QSR supports counter ordering, held bills and payment workflows. Both connect order activity to kitchen workflows.

Kitchen and order handling

KOTs route orders to kitchen stations. Cloud Kitchen and kitchen display workflows help teams manage incoming orders and preparation.

Digital ordering channels

Table QR, Captain App, Website Ordering and App Ordering provide customer and staff-facing ordering experiences. Supported Swiggy and Zomato orders can be brought into the POS workflow.

Account and cloud services

The customer portal is used for account access, licences, downloads and support requests. POSINEXT Cloud supports online orders, backups and licences.
