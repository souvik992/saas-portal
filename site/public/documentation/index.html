Start with your restaurant service model

Before configuring a restaurant POS, identify how orders are created and fulfilled at your outlet. Fine-dine operations use tables and waiter order entry. Quick-service restaurants focus on counter billing. Cloud kitchens coordinate incoming digital orders and preparation. POSINEXT provides different workflows for these models.

Read the module overview for Fine Dine POS, QSR POS and Cloud Kitchen before selecting a configuration.

Understand the order-to-kitchen workflow

At a high level, a staff member or customer submits an order through a supported POS or ordering channel. The order enters the outlet workflow and can proceed to kitchen order ticket (KOT) handling and preparation. The exact steps depend on the module and configuration. When evaluating the system, check how item modifiers, special instructions, changes and cancellations are represented at each stage.

POSINEXT module guide

Point of sale

Fine Dine covers table service, floor visibility, order entry and billing. QSR covers menu-led counter ordering and held-bill workflows.

Kitchen and staff mobility

Captain App supports mobile order entry by service staff. Cloud Kitchen focuses on digital order handling and kitchen preparation across food brands.

Customer ordering channels

Table QR, Website Ordering, App Ordering and the Food Delivery App provide additional customer-facing channels. Supported Swiggy and Zomato integration can connect aggregator orders with the POS workflow.

Account access, licences and support

Customers can sign in to the POSINEXT customer portal to access account features, manage licences and open support requests. POSINEXT Cloud supports online orders, backups and licences. The Windows outlet POS supports offline operation for supported local workflows; online services require connectivity.

Information to include in a support request

When asking for help, describe the module, outlet workflow, approximate time and steps that led to the issue. Include screenshots only when they help explain the problem, and remove customer personal information, passwords, access tokens and payment details. For account or product assistance, contact support@posinext.com.

Need setup help for a particular restaurant workflow? Contact the POSINEXT team with your module and outlet details.
