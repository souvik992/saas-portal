QR code table ordering, straight into your POS

Every table gets its own QR code. Guests scan it with their phone camera to see your menu, order, call a waiter or ask for the bill, with no app to download. Orders land in the POS with the table attached and KOTs print in the kitchen.

What you get

Table QR features

A code for every table

Codes are made per table, with a ready-to-print sheet for table tents and stickers.

Digital menu with photos

Guests see the same menu, prices and photos as your POS, so there is nothing to update twice.

Staff or automatic accepting

Choose whether staff approve each QR order, or it is accepted and sent to the kitchen straight away.

Call a waiter, ask for the bill

A tap on the guest's phone alerts your staff in the POS, with the table marked on the floor plan.

Running bill and UPI

Guests see their running bill and can pay by UPI; the cashier confirms the payment in the POS.

Works on mobile data

Codes can open through PosiNext Cloud, so guests on mobile data can order, not only those on your Wi-Fi.

How it works

Up and running in four steps

1. Turn on Table QR

Install the Table QR add-on with Fine Dine (DinePOS) and switch it on in Settings.

2. Print your codes

Print the sheet of codes, one per table, and place them on the tables.

3. Guests scan and order

Guests open the menu with their camera, order and call for service from their phone.

4. Kitchen gets the KOT

Orders reach the POS with the table attached and print at the right kitchen station.

FAQ

Table QR FAQ

Do guests need to download an app?

No. The QR code opens the menu in the phone's browser.

Does QR ordering work with my POS?

Table QR is an add-on for POSINEXT Fine Dine (DinePOS). Orders appear on the floor plan and KOTs print like any other order.

Can staff check QR orders before they go to the kitchen?

Yes. Choose staff accepting, and each QR order waits for a staff member to accept it. Or choose automatic accepting to send it straight through.

How do guests pay?

Guests see their running bill and can pay by UPI; the cashier confirms the payment. They can also pay at the counter as usual.

What does Table QR cost?

Table QR ordering and waiter calling are included in the Growth plan, ₹18,000 per outlet per year plus GST. See pricing.
