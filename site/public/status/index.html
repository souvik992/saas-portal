Report a service or product issue

Email support@posinext.com with the service affected, the approximate time of the issue, the outlet or account reference and the steps needed to reproduce it. Include whether the problem affects local POS billing, online orders, licence access or the customer portal.

Understand offline and online services

The Windows outlet POS is designed to continue working offline for supported local workflows. Online orders, cloud services, backups, licences and customer portal functions may require an active internet connection. When reporting a problem, identify whether local billing continues to work and which internet-dependent service is affected.

Protect account information when contacting support

Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes, access tokens or full payment card details. Remove customer personal information from screenshots before sharing them. If a report includes a transaction, share only the minimum reference needed for the team to identify it.
