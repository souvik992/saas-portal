Bring order taking closer to the guest

In a full-service restaurant, waiters move between tables while the billing terminal may be in another part of the outlet. A waiter order-taking app can reduce that back-and-forth by giving staff a mobile interface for selecting the table, browsing menu items and entering order details where the guest is seated.

POSINEXT Captain App is designed for this staff-facing job. It complements the main POS instead of replacing the need for outlet billing and kitchen operations.

Capture menu items and modifiers accurately

Guests often request changes to a standard item: a particular size, add-on or preparation preference. Staff need to select the right item and its available modifiers before submitting the order. During a demo, test the menu structure and modifier combinations used by your restaurant, then check how the captured details appear in the order and kitchen workflow.

Connect waiter orders with KOT and billing

A mobile order should remain part of the same order record used by the outlet. POSINEXT Captain App submits orders into the restaurant's connected POS workflow, where order details can move through the configured kitchen ticket process. The wider Fine Dine POS experience covers table visibility and billing, while the platform overview explains other connected modules.

Captain App rollout checklist

  • Confirm the phones or tablets that staff will use and the outlet network conditions.
  • Review how waiters sign in and identify the correct table.
  • Test common menu items, modifiers and special order scenarios.
  • Verify where submitted orders appear and how the kitchen receives them.
  • Check how staff request bill preparation and how the cashier completes payment.

Waiter app FAQs

What is a restaurant captain app?

A captain app is a mobile tool used by restaurant service staff to take orders at guest tables and send them to the outlet POS and kitchen workflow.

Can waiters take orders from a phone?

POSINEXT Captain App is designed for mobile order taking on a phone or tablet. Confirm the supported device and deployment requirements for your outlet.

Does the Captain App replace restaurant POS software?

No. It is a staff mobility add-on that works with the connected POS workflow for order management, kitchen handling and billing.
