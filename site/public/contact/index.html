What to include in a restaurant POS enquiry

A little context helps the team prepare a useful product discussion. When contacting POSINEXT, include your restaurant format, city, number of outlets and approximate number of billing terminals. Mention whether you need table service, QSR counter billing, cloud kitchen operations, KOT workflows, Table QR, website ordering, app ordering or supported Swiggy and Zomato integration.

You can also describe the main challenge you want to solve, such as managing table orders, routing tickets to the kitchen or handling orders from several channels. Avoid sending passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information.

What happens during a product walkthrough?

A useful demo should follow the way your restaurant operates. You can review order entry, menu and modifier selection, KOT handling, billing and the ordering channels relevant to your team. Bring a sample menu or describe a typical order so you can evaluate the workflow rather than only viewing a generic feature tour.

Frequently asked questions

How can I request a POSINEXT demo?

Can I ask for pricing without a demo?

Yes. Share your outlet and terminal requirements and the features you need. Ask for a written proposal that identifies the included modules and commercial terms.
