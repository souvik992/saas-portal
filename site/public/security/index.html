Security

Contact and reporting guidance for security concerns involving POSINEXT.

Draft for owner review · Prepared 6 November 2026

This draft is provided by POSINEXT for review. It is not a finalized policy or service agreement. Contact support@posinext.com with questions.

1. Report a concern

To raise a suspected website security issue, email support@posinext.com with the subject “POSINEXT security concern”. This is the general POSINEXT contact, not a dedicated emergency channel.

2. What to include

Describe the affected page, what you observed, when it happened and the steps needed to reproduce it. If you include screenshots, remove personal details, passwords, tokens and payment information first. Do not send another person's data as evidence.

3. Keep reporting safe

Do not access, download or alter someone else's information. Do not disrupt services or continue an investigation that risks harm. This page does not authorize security testing, establish a bug bounty or grant legal safe harbor.

4. Protect your own information

Never share passwords, payment credentials or one-time codes through the demo form or an unsolicited message. If a message claiming to be from POSINEXT looks suspicious, contact the team separately before following its instructions.

5. Security information for buyers

Before selecting software, ask for current information about access controls, backups, hosting, encryption and support arrangements relevant to your deployment. This page does not attest to any certification, audit result, regulatory compliance status or specific technical security control.

6. Scope and review

This owner-authored draft is not independent verification or a security guarantee. Reporting procedures and any response commitments require confirmation by the site owner; no response time or incident-resolution promise is made here.
