Work on software used in day-to-day operations

Restaurant software has to work in the middle of a real service: orders are being entered, bills are being closed, kitchen tickets are moving and staff need clear information. Building for this environment calls for attention to usability, reliability and the details of how people work. POSINEXT's product areas span Windows-based outlet POS workflows, web applications, mobile experiences, cloud services and integrations.

Areas of work at POSINEXT

Engineering and quality

Product engineering includes web interfaces, backend services, integrations and testing. Quality work is closely connected to real user journeys: order capture, billing, table service, kitchen handoff, online ordering and account management.

Product and design

Product and design work starts with understanding restaurant operations and turning those needs into clear workflows. Useful skills include user research, interaction design, writing requirements and maintaining product documentation.

Customer success and support

Customer-facing work helps restaurant teams understand the product, prepare their setup and get assistance when they have a question. Clear communication and practical problem-solving are important.

How to send a career enquiry

Send a short introduction to support@posinext.com with the subject “Career enquiry”. Include the role or area you are interested in, a brief summary of relevant experience and links to your portfolio, GitHub or professional profile where applicable. You may attach a CV. Please do not include passwords, identity documents or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

This page does not list live vacancies. Sending an enquiry does not mean a role is currently open or guarantee a response or interview.
