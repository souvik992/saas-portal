QSR billing software built for counter speed

POSINEXT QSR (Tiffin POS) is a Windows billing app for quick-service restaurants, cafés, bakeries, tiffin centres and cloud kitchens. A photo-first menu, one-tap add and cash, UPI and card in one panel keep the queue moving, online or offline.

What you get

QSR features

Photo-grid menu

Category tabs and dish photos, so new staff find items fast. Mark a dish sold out in a tap.

GST bills in seconds

GST-ready bills with split payments, held bills, shifts and credit notes, in a bill design with your logo for 58 or 80 mm printers.

Printing that never loses a ticket

USB, LAN and Wi-Fi receipt printers. Bills and KOTs wait in a print queue and print in order, even after a printer was off.

KOTs and kitchen display

KOTs go straight to the kitchen printer or screen, so cooking starts while the customer pays.

Stock and recipes

Raw materials, purchases and wastage, with recipes that use up stock as you sell.

Offline, with backups

No server and no internet needed to bill. Reports and automatic backups are built in.

How it works

Up and running in four steps

1. Start your free trial

Create an account in the customer portal and choose QSR. The 30-day trial needs no card.

2. Install Tiffin POS

Download the Windows installer and install it on the counter PC. No server, no setup visit needed.

3. Add menu and printers

Add categories, dishes and photos, then plug in or add your receipt and kitchen printers.

4. Start billing

Ring up orders, take cash, UPI or card, and send KOTs to the kitchen.

Works together

Pairs well with

Swiggy & Zomato

Our own Swiggy and Zomato integration, built into the POS. Orders are accepted automatically and KOTs print in the kitchen the moment they arrive, with no extra tablets on the counter.

FAQ

QSR FAQ

Does the QSR billing software work offline?

Yes. Tiffin POS bills with no internet and no server. Online orders and online backups catch up when the connection is back.

Is it right for a café, bakery or tiffin centre?

Yes. QSR is made for counter service of any kind: quick-service restaurants, cafés, bakeries, tiffin centres, food courts and cloud kitchens.

Can I take Swiggy and Zomato orders?

Yes, with the ODS screen: website, app, Swiggy and Zomato orders appear on one screen and KOTs print automatically. See Swiggy & Zomato integration.

Which printers work?

58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipt printers by USB, LAN or Wi-Fi. A USB printer can be set up from the billing screen in one tap.

How much does it cost?

From ₹10,800 per outlet per year plus GST, after a 30-day free trial. See pricing.
