Software for delivery-focused kitchen operations

A cloud kitchen may prepare food for several virtual brands from the same physical kitchen. Orders can arrive through different channels, but the team still needs to understand what to prepare, which brand an order belongs to and where it is in the kitchen workflow. Cloud kitchen software should make this operational picture easier to manage rather than adding another disconnected inbox.

POSINEXT Cloud Kitchen brings supported order activity into connected kitchen operations. The right configuration should be reviewed against the number of brands, order channels, preparation stations and staff roles in your business.

Keep multi-brand order handling organised

When multiple food brands share preparation space, a clear order identity matters. Staff need to distinguish orders, check the requested items and prepare them in the right sequence. Review how brand menus and incoming orders are represented in your POSINEXT setup, and confirm how the kitchen team will identify each ticket during peak periods.

Connect digital channels with kitchen preparation

Cloud kitchens may receive orders from a restaurant's own digital channels as well as supported third-party platforms. POSINEXT includes options for website ordering, app ordering and supported Swiggy and Zomato integration. Ask the team to demonstrate the exact order lifecycle—from channel intake and acceptance through KOT and preparation—for each channel you plan to use.

What to evaluate before choosing cloud kitchen software

  • How many brands and menus will the kitchen operate?
  • Which order channels need to connect to the outlet POS?
  • How are incoming orders presented to preparation staff?
  • Can the team identify modifiers and special instructions on the ticket?
  • What happens if internet connectivity is interrupted?
  • Which reports, permissions and operational controls are available in your proposed configuration?

Cloud kitchen software FAQs

What is cloud kitchen management software?

It is software used to coordinate orders and food preparation for delivery-focused kitchens, including operations where multiple virtual brands share a kitchen.

Can POSINEXT support multiple food brands?

POSINEXT Cloud Kitchen is positioned for kitchen operations across multiple food brands. Confirm brand limits and the exact configuration for your business during a product demo.

Can aggregator orders reach the kitchen workflow?

POSINEXT supports connected workflows for supported aggregator orders. Confirm the channels, account prerequisites and end-to-end kitchen flow required for your setup.
