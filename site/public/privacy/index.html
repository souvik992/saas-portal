Privacy notice

Information about enquiries made through the POSINEXT website.

Draft for owner review · Prepared 6 November 2026

This draft is provided by POSINEXT for review. It is not a finalized policy or service agreement. Contact support@posinext.com with questions.

1. Scope of this notice

This draft covers the public POSINEXT website and its demo request form. It does not describe how a restaurant processes its guests' information or establish the privacy terms of a purchased POSINEXT service.

2. Information in a demo request

The form asks for your name, phone number and restaurant name. You can also provide your email, city, number of outlets and a message. Please avoid including payment details, passwords or personal information about restaurant guests in your enquiry.

3. Enquiry handling

Demo requests are saved and sent to the POSINEXT team so we can respond to your enquiry. Submitting the form requests contact about POSINEXT; it is not a purchase or subscription.

4. WhatsApp and other external services

If you choose the WhatsApp link, you leave this website and communicate through WhatsApp. Review that service's privacy information before sending personal details. This draft does not provide a complete list of website service providers or their data-processing terms.

5. Privacy questions and requests

For questions about an enquiry, or to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, email support@posinext.com. Include enough detail to identify your enquiry, but do not send passwords or sensitive documents. This draft does not promise a response deadline or a particular outcome.

6. Details awaiting confirmation

The site owner needs to confirm the legal business identity, applicable privacy requirements, retention periods, provider arrangements, and cookie or analytics practices before adopting a final notice. No retention period, legal compliance status or data-sharing guarantee is stated here.
