What affects restaurant POS software cost?

Restaurant POS pricing is not only a per-terminal question. The total setup can depend on the number of outlets, the number of billing terminals, the type of service operation and which additional ordering or kitchen modules are required. Hardware, installation, onboarding, support coverage and any third-party integration charges should also be clarified in the written proposal.

POSINEXT does not publish a universal price on this page because the right configuration depends on your requirements. Contact the team for a quote that identifies the modules and commercial terms relevant to your restaurant.

Questions to ask before comparing POS quotes

  • Which modules are included in the base licence, and which are add-ons?
  • Is the quote per outlet, per terminal, per user or a combination?
  • What hardware and printer models are supported for the proposed setup?
  • Which workflows continue during an internet interruption, and how does sync work?
  • What onboarding, training, support and renewal terms are included?
  • Are integrations or payment-related services subject to separate fees?

What to share when requesting a quote

To make the discussion useful, share your restaurant format, city, number of outlets, approximate number of POS terminals, menu complexity and the channels you use for orders. Mention whether you need dine-in table service, QSR counter billing, cloud kitchen workflows, Table QR, your own website or app, or Swiggy and Zomato integration.

Restaurant POS pricing FAQs

Does POSINEXT show fixed pricing online?

No. This page invites you to request a proposal based on your restaurant's outlets, terminals and selected modules. Ask for an itemised quote before making a decision.

Can I start with POS and add ordering modules later?

POSINEXT has a core POS platform and additional ordering channels. Discuss your phased rollout and module availability with the team so the proposal reflects your intended setup.

Are hardware and onboarding included?

These details depend on the commercial proposal. Ask the team to state hardware, installation, onboarding, training and support inclusions in writing.
