Which restaurant POS plan should I choose?

Base suits an outlet needing core billing, menu, stock and reports. Growth adds waiter ordering, kitchen display, Table QR and online ordering integration. Scale includes additional on-site installation and training visits and priority support.

Frequently asked questions about POS pricing

Are these prices monthly or yearly?

They are annual prices per outlet, before GST.

Is GST included?

No. GST is additional to the displayed annual plan price.

Is there a free trial?

The customer portal supports a 30-day trial.

Does POSINEXT work without internet?

The Windows POS at the outlet is designed to work offline. Online orders, cloud backups and licence operations require connectivity to POSINEXT Cloud.

Can I use POSINEXT for fine dine or QSR?

Yes. Base includes Fine Dine (DinePOS) or QSR (Tiffin POS).
