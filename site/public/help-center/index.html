Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Create an account at the registration page. The portal supports a 30-day trial.

Where can I download the POS?

Sign in to the customer portal and use the Downloads area for installers published for your account and product.

How do I get support?

Create a support ticket with the product, issue, steps to reproduce and relevant screenshots. Avoid passwords, payment credentials or guest personal data.

What if my outlet internet is down?

The Windows POS is designed for offline billing at the outlet. Cloud-connected functions such as online orders, cloud backups and licence operations need connectivity.

How can I report a security concern?

Follow the guidance on the security page.
