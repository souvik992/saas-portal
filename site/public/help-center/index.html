Getting started with POSINEXT

Customers can access account features, downloads, licences and support requests through the POSINEXT customer portal. If you are evaluating the platform for a new restaurant, begin with the module overview and request a product walkthrough to review your service model.

Before reporting a POS or billing issue

Identify the outlet and terminal affected, the approximate time the issue occurred and the steps that led to it. Note whether the problem affects one order or multiple transactions, and whether it can be reproduced. If the issue involves a bill or payment, do not email full card numbers, credentials or other sensitive payment data.

Questions about kitchen tickets and order flow

For a KOT or kitchen issue, include the order channel, the item or modifier involved, the preparation station and what the staff expected to happen. For digital orders, identify whether the order came from Table QR, website ordering, app ordering or a supported aggregator integration. This context helps distinguish an order-entry question from a kitchen routing or channel setup question.

Connectivity and offline operation

The Windows outlet POS is designed to support offline operation for supported local workflows. Online orders, backups, licences and other cloud services may require internet connectivity. When reporting a connectivity issue, include whether local POS billing is working, whether the internet connection is available and which cloud-dependent function is affected.

How to contact support safely

Email support@posinext.com or use the customer portal where available. Screenshots or short screen recordings can help explain a workflow, but first remove customer personal information, passwords, one-time codes, access tokens and payment details. Never send your password to support.

Help center FAQs

Where do I manage my POSINEXT licence?

Sign in to the customer portal to access account and licence features.

What should I include in a support email?

Include the affected module, outlet or terminal, approximate time, steps to reproduce and what you expected to happen. Do not include passwords or sensitive payment data.

Where can I learn about POSINEXT modules?

See the product documentation overview and restaurant POS features page.
