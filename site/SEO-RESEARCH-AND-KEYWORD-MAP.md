Prepared: 13 November 2026
Market: India
Audience: restaurant owners, operators, QSR teams, cloud-kitchen operators and restaurant technology evaluators
Objective: qualified product-demo enquiries for POSINEXT restaurant POS and ordering workflows

## Research approach

This plan combines Google Search Central guidance, a manual review of India-focused restaurant POS search-result themes, and the actual POSINEXT modules and workflows described on the website and in product materials.

Google recommends useful, people-first content, descriptive page titles, crawlable internal links and structured data that accurately reflects visible page content. It does not prescribe a preferred word count or guarantee rankings from a particular SEO change. The implementation therefore targets distinct user intents and operational questions rather than a word-count target.

## Keyword ownership and cannibalisation guardrails

1. The homepage owns the broad category phrase restaurant POS software. Add-on pages should not all target the same broad phrase.
2. Fine Dine and QSR have distinct service-model intents: table-led dining versus counter-led quick service.
3. Features explains platform breadth; individual product pages explain one module and its operating workflow.
4. Pricing owns cost and quote intent. Do not publish a fixed amount until the commercial offer is approved and stable.
5. Table QR, Captain App, Cloud Kitchen and the direct-ordering pages each own their module-specific intents.
6. Food Delivery App describes the branded customer app proposition; App Ordering focuses on mobile order submission and its connection to outlet operations. Monitor their query overlap in Search Console and consolidate only if data shows they compete for the same intent.
7. Swiggy/Zomato content must describe supported integration only. Do not imply menu sync, stock sync, payout reconciliation or automatic order acceptance unless those functions are verified for the exact integration and account configuration.

## Implementation guardrails

- Keep one canonical URL per page and ensure sitemap URLs match those canonicals.
- Keep indexable landing pages crawlable and reachable through descriptive internal links.
- Use a clear H1 and useful heading hierarchy. Avoid repeating generic paragraphs across multiple pages.
- Titles and meta descriptions should be unique and accurate. Google may still generate a different title link or snippet in search results.
- Structured data must match information visible on the page. Do not add fabricated ratings, reviews, prices, certifications or performance statistics.
- Do not add SoftwareApplication rich-result markup until all required fields are genuinely available and consistent with the visible offer. No fabricated price has been added.
- Do not add FAQ rich-result markup just because a page contains FAQs; the FAQ copy is currently written to help visitors understand the product.
- Do not create city-specific doorway pages without genuinely distinct local service coverage, proof and useful local information.
- Do not publish customer counts, uptime SLAs, support hours, onboarding promises, testimonials, certifications or integration capabilities unless the business can substantiate them.
- Do not treat keyword density or an arbitrary word count as a ranking objective.

## Post-deployment measurement plan

### Google Search Console
1. Verify the posinext.com domain property.
2. Submit https://posinext.com/sitemap.xml.
3. Inspect the homepage and priority product URLs; confirm Google's selected canonical matches the intended canonical.
4. Monitor indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by page and query.
5. Review query overlap between /food-delivery-app and /app-ordering, and between the homepage and /features.

### Google Keyword Planner
Use a separate India-targeted Search campaign research pass. Set location to India and language to English initially. Consider Hindi or other regional languages only when matching landing-page content and sales coverage exist. Group seed terms separately: restaurant POS, restaurant billing, fine-dine POS, QSR POS, cloud kitchen POS, QR menu ordering, waiter app, restaurant online ordering, food delivery app, and Swiggy/Zomato POS integration.

Export monthly search ranges, competition and top-of-page bid ranges from the account. Keep brand, non-brand, informational and high-intent commercial terms in separate groups. Exact volumes and CPCs are not stated in this document because they have not been measured in Keyword Planner; do not invent these values.

### Performance and conversion
- Test representative pages with PageSpeed Insights on mobile and desktop.
- Review real-user Core Web Vitals when enough field data is available. Google's good thresholds are LCP <= 2.5 seconds, INP < 200 milliseconds and CLS < 0.1.
- Measure demo enquiries and qualified contact actions; distinguish successful leads from simple CTA clicks.
- Review organic landing-page conversions, not traffic alone.
- Refresh copy when product capabilities or commercial terms change, and only change publication dates when there is a meaningful update.
