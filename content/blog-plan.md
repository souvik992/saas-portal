# POSINEXT blog plan

The daily blog task reads this file, writes the first unchecked topic as a CMS post in `content/posts/<slug>.json`,
ticks it, and adds a line to the log. Edit freely: reorder, add or remove topics. Posts can also be written by hand in
https://posinext.com/admin/.

Goal: bring restaurant owners and managers in India (fine dine, QSR, cafés, cloud kitchens, bakeries, tiffin services)
from Google, Bing and AI assistants to posinext.com, and give them a reason to book a demo or start the free trial.
Each post goes deep on one channel or problem and links back to the pillar guide
`/blog/restaurant-marketing-channels-india/`.

## Rules for every post

- One search intent per post. Title under 70 characters with the main keyword near the start; meta description
  140–160 characters; slug short, lowercase, hyphenated, unique.
- 1,500–2,500 words of original, practical content an owner can act on this week: steps, checklists, a table, a worked
  example with ₹ figures clearly marked as illustrative.
- Every statistic, rate, rule or platform programme comes from a source found and read that day (prefer government,
  regulator, platform help pages, NCAER/NRAI/Redseer, major business press). Name the source in the text and list it
  under a final "Sources" heading with links. Never invent numbers, quotes, case studies or customer stories.
- Say when something varies by city, contract or date. Tax, legal and food-safety topics: point to the official source
  (GST: cbic.gov.in, FSSAI: fssai.gov.in) and add a one-line "general information, not legal/tax advice" note.
- 3–6 internal links where they genuinely help: the pillar guide, related posts, and product pages (/qsr-pos/,
  /fine-dine-pos/, /table-qr-ordering/, /captain-app/, /cloud-kitchen-software/, /online-ordering-website/,
  /app-ordering/, /food-delivery-app/, /swiggy-zomato-integration/, /features/, /pricing/, /book-a-demo/).
  Mention POSINEXT once or twice where it fits; the post must be useful even to someone who never buys.
- 4–6 FAQs answering real follow-up questions in 1–3 sentences each.
- Allowed HTML in body_html: p h2 h3 ul ol li strong em blockquote table thead tbody tr th td a br. No h1, images,
  classes, scripts or inline styles.
